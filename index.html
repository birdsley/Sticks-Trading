Trading Research Suite

A quantitative experiment identifying the limits of informational edge on point-in-time daily S&P 500 OHLCV data. Rather than competing on execution latency, the suite tests whether structural signals extracted from graph topology, portfolio theory, and reinforcement learning remain statistically significant at daily resolution, and what their failure modes reveal about current market microstructure.

Three Strategies
Sticks TGA Daily  ·  Mean Reversion
Builds a Minimum Spanning Tree on the correlation-distance matrix of S&P 500 daily returns, then measures how far each stock's return has "diffused" from its expected MST position. The residual is the tradeable signal — stocks that drift far from the graph mean-revert on a 3–7 day horizon. Entry gated by z-score threshold and XGBoost probability, sized by ATR-inverse volatility parity.
S&P 500 PIT Universe MST Heat Diffusion MOO T+1 Fills · 7 bps Backtest Sharpe +0.602
Markowitz Weekly  ·  Portfolio Optimization
Classical mean-variance optimization across S&P 500 stocks and commodity futures (gold, silver, crude, natgas, corn, wheat, soy). Estimates a Ledoit-Wolf shrunk covariance matrix over a 252-day rolling window, then solves for maximum Sharpe subject to long-only, full-investment, and 10% per-position constraints. Rebalances every Monday at market open.
S&P 500 + Commodities Ledoit-Wolf Shrinkage Max 10% Position Weekly Rebalance
DRL Vol Daily  ·  Reinforcement Learning
A Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agent trained to target consistent risk-adjusted returns across 9 diversifying ETFs. The agent learns a nonlinear policy for risk scaling — compressing exposure in high-vol regimes, expanding in calm markets — subject to per-asset turnover constraints and a quadratic turnover penalty that discourages excessive churn.
9-ETF Fixed Universe PPO · Walk-Forward Backtest Sharpe 0.730 Regime Adaptive
MST Network — Topological Graph Arbitrage
What We're Testing
Institutional infrastructure trades at sub-millisecond latency. Operating on daily bars is a deliberate constraint — the question is not whether we can beat HFT, but whether structural signals remain statistically significant across macroeconomic regimes, and what their failure modes reveal about market efficiency.
Hypothesis 1

Graph Topology as Alpha

Cross-sectional deviations from MST-predicted positions encode temporary mispricing that mean-reverts on a 3–7 day horizon. The topological signal fails gracefully — if it doesn't work, the residuals scatter randomly rather than reversing, giving a clean null signal.

Hypothesis 2

Regime Non-Stationarity

Markets switch between structural states (bull, bear, crisis, neutral) detectable from cross-sectional correlation and trailing momentum. Allocating across regimes rather than blindly extrapolating should extract diversification alpha beyond naïve buy-and-hold.

Hypothesis 3

RL as Risk Budget

A PPO agent can learn a nonlinear risk-scaling policy that outperforms simple volatility-targeting heuristics. The controlled 9-ETF universe isolates the learning problem and avoids survivorship bias — the agent's failures are interpretable and informative.
