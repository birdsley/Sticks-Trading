Trading Research Suite

This quantitative experiment analyzes the limits of informational edge using point-in-time daily S&P 500 OHLCV data. Instead of focusing on execution latency, the research suite evaluates whether structural signals derived from graph topology, portfolio theory, and reinforcement learning retain statistical significance at daily resolution. Additionally, it examines what the failure modes of these signals reveal about contemporary market microstructure.

Four Strategies
Sticks TGA Daily  ·  Mean Reversion
This strategy constructs a Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) on the correlation-distance matrix of S&P 500 daily returns and quantifies the extent to which each stock's return deviates from its expected MST position. The resulting residual serves as the tradable signal; stocks that differ substantially from the graph tend to mean-revert over a 3 to 7 day horizon. Trade entry is determined by a z-score threshold and XGBoost probability, with position sizing based on ATR-inverse volatility parity.
S&P 500 PIT Universe MST Heat Diffusion MOO T+1 Fills · 7 bps Backtest Sharpe +0.602
Trend + Carry Monthly  ·  Cross-Asset  ·  Live since 2026-06-02
A diversified cross-asset book on ~25 liquid ETFs spanning equity indices, Treasuries, credit, commodities and FX. Two complementary sleeves are risk-parity blended: a time-series momentum (trend) sleeve that goes long assets in positive trends and short those in negative ones across 3/6/12-month horizons, and a long-only carry/defensive sleeve (Treasuries, credit, TIPS, gold, REITs) tilted by the Treasury yield-curve slope (10y−3m), damping duration when the curve inverts. On top, a gradient-boosted (XGBoost) meta-model sizes or vetoes each trend bet, and a regime overlay trims exposure when markets turn risk-off — which roughly halves drawdowns — with the whole book held to a steady volatility target. The blend stays positive in every major crisis window and keeps near-zero correlation to the S&P.
~25-ETF Cross-Asset Trend + Yield-Curve Carry Validated Sharpe +1.03 Corr to S&P −0.04 · Crisis-Positive
Markowitz Weekly  ·  Portfolio Optimization
This approach applies classical mean-variance optimization to a universe comprising S&P 500 stocks and selected commodity futures (gold, silver, crude oil, natural gas, corn, wheat, and soy). It estimates a Ledoit-Wolf-shrunk covariance matrix over a 252-day rolling window and solves for the maximum Sharpe ratio, subject to long-only, full-investment, and 10% per-position constraints. The portfolio is rebalanced weekly at the market open.
S&P 500 + Commodities Ledoit-Wolf Shrinkage Max 10% Position Weekly Rebalance
DRL Vol Daily  ·  Reinforcement Learning
A Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agent is trained to achieve consistent risk-adjusted returns across nine diversifying ETFs. The agent develops a nonlinear policy for risk scaling, reducing exposure during periods of high volatility and increasing it in stable market conditions. This process is subject to per-asset turnover constraints and incorporates a quadratic turnover penalty to restrain excessive trading activity.
9-ETF Fixed Universe PPO · Walk-Forward Backtest Sharpe 0.730 Regime Adaptive
MST Network — Topological Graph Arbitrage
What We're Testing
Institutional trading infrastructure operates at sub-millisecond latency. The use of daily bars is an unwanted yet intentional constraint. The main goal is not to outperform high-frequency trading, but to determine whether structural signals retain statistical significance throughout various macroeconomic regimes and to analyze what their failure modes indicate about market efficiency.
Hypothesis 1

Graph Topology as Alpha

Cross-sectional variations from MST-predicted positions are thought to represent temporary mispricing that mean-reverts over a 3 to 7 day horizon. The topological signal is expected to fail gracefully; when ineffective, the residuals disperse randomly rather than reversing, thereby yielding a clear null signal.

Hypothesis 2

Regime Non-Stationarity

Markets transition among structural states, such as bull, bear, crisis, and neutral, which can be identified through cross-sectional correlation and trailing momentum. Allocating assets based on regime identification, rather than relying on simple extrapolation, is expected to generate diversification alpha beyond that of a naïve buy-and-hold strategy.

Hypothesis 3

RL as Risk Budget

A PPO agent is believed to learn a nonlinear risk-scaling policy that surpasses the performance of basic volatility-targeting heuristics. The use of a controlled nine-ETF universe isolates the learning problem and mitigates survivorship bias, making certain that the agent's failures remain interpretable and informative.

Hypothesis 4

Diversification as the Premium

Single-name statistical edges on the S&P have decayed, but cross-asset time-series momentum and carry remain robust on daily data. Blending two lowly-correlated sleeves (trend + carry) across ~25 ETFs is expected to deliver a credible, crisis-positive risk-adjusted return uncorrelated to equities — the diversification premium, not single-stock alpha, is the durable signal under a daily-bar constraint.
