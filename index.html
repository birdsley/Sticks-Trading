ALPHA EXPERIMENT DAILY BARS · PAPER TRADING SINCE 2026-06-02

Trading Research Suite

This project tests the limits of informational edge in point-in-time daily S&P 500 OHLCV data. It does not compete on execution speed. It asks whether structural signals from graph topology, portfolio theory and reinforcement learning keep their statistical significance at daily resolution, and what the ways they fail say about market microstructure.

Three Strategies
Sticks TGA Daily  ·  Mean Reversion
This strategy builds a Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) on the correlation-distance matrix of S&P 500 daily returns and measures how far each stock's return sits from its expected MST position. That residual is the trading signal: stocks that stray far from the graph tend to mean-revert over 3 to 7 days. Trade entry is determined by a z-score threshold and XGBoost probability, with position sizing based on ATR-inverse volatility parity.
S&P 500 PIT Universe MST Heat Diffusion MOO T+1 Fills · 7 bps Backtest Sharpe +0.602
Trend + Carry Monthly  ·  Cross-Asset  ·  Live since 2026-06-02
A diversified cross-asset book on ~23 liquid ETFs spanning equity indices, Treasuries, credit, commodities and FX. Two complementary sleeves are risk-parity blended: a time-series momentum (trend) sleeve that goes long assets in positive trends and short those in negative ones, sized by how statistically strong each trend is (the Newey-West t-statistic of returns over 3, 6 and 12 months, capped, following Baltas & Kosowski) rather than by its sign alone, and a long-only carry/defensive sleeve (Treasuries, credit, TIPS, gold, REITs) tilted by the Treasury yield-curve slope (10y−3m), damping duration when the curve inverts. On top, a gradient-boosted (XGBoost) meta-model sizes or vetoes each trend bet, and a regime overlay trims exposure when markets turn risk-off, which roughly halves drawdowns. The whole book is held to a steady volatility target. The book loses little in crisis windows (worst −6.4%, the 2020 COVID crash) and keeps a low correlation to the S&P.
~23-ETF Cross-Asset Trend + Yield-Curve Carry Backtest Sharpe +1.24 · 2007–2026 · 8% vol Corr to S&P +0.17 · MaxDD −12.5%
Markowitz Weekly  ·  Portfolio Optimization
Classical mean-variance optimization over S&P 500 stocks and a set of commodity futures (gold, silver, crude oil, natural gas, corn, wheat, and soy). It estimates a Ledoit-Wolf-shrunk covariance matrix over a 252-day rolling window and solves for the maximum Sharpe ratio, subject to long-only, full-investment, and 10% per-position constraints. The portfolio is rebalanced weekly at the market open.

MST network: topological graph arbitrage
What we're testing
Institutional trading systems run at sub-millisecond latency. We use daily bars on purpose, even though they are a handicap. The goal is to find out whether structural signals stay statistically significant across macroeconomic regimes, and what their failures say about market efficiency, rather than to beat high-frequency trading.
Hypothesis 1

Graph topology as alpha

Cross-sectional variations from MST-predicted positions are thought to represent temporary mispricing that mean-reverts over a 3 to 7 day horizon. The topological signal is expected to fail gracefully; when ineffective, the residuals disperse randomly rather than reversing, which gives a clear null signal.

Hypothesis 2

Regime non-stationarity

Markets transition among structural states, such as bull, bear, crisis, and neutral, which can be identified through cross-sectional correlation and trailing momentum. Allocating assets based on regime identification, rather than relying on simple extrapolation, is expected to generate diversification alpha beyond that of a plain buy-and-hold strategy.

Hypothesis 3 · Retired

RL as a risk budget

A PPO agent was expected to learn a nonlinear risk-scaling policy that beats basic volatility-targeting heuristics on a controlled nine-ETF universe. It did not: fed correctly, its live weights stayed within 2% of equal-weight. The strategy was retired in October 2026 and its defensive role passed to Trend + Carry. Deep RL may return with a richer state space and a broader universe.

Hypothesis 4

Diversification as the premium

Single-name statistical edges on the S&P have decayed, but cross-asset time-series momentum and carry still hold up on daily data. Blending two sleeves with low correlation to each other (trend + carry) across ~25 ETFs is expected to deliver a credible, crisis-positive risk-adjusted return uncorrelated to equities. Under a daily-bar constraint, the lasting signal is the diversification premium rather than single-stock alpha.

POWER MARKETS · ERCOT 1 MW / 4 MWh · ONE CYCLE / DAY · WALK-FORWARD OOS

A forecast that decides when a battery buys and sells power.

Every evening a gradient-boosted model forecasts tomorrow’s 24 day-ahead prices. The battery charges in the four cheapest forecast hours and discharges in the four most expensive, and each trade is scored against the price that settled. This tab measures how much of the perfect-insight profit the forecast captures, and how much better that is than running the battery on a fixed schedule.

Dispatch fingerprint

CHARGE DISCHARGE
Winter. Two discharge windows, early morning (05:00 to 07:00) and evening (17:00 to 20:00), with charging moved to midday.
Spring. Charging sits in the late morning (09:00 to 12:00) and discharge moves later, to 19:00 to 21:00.
June. Charge 08:00 to 11:00, discharge 19:00 to 21:00 on almost every day. The window ends in June, so this is one month of summer.

Other results

Battery dispatch: profitability against the fixed schedule and perfect insight

Annualised $/MW-yr and risk-adjusted metrics per dispatch rule. signal_p50 is the deployed rule; the fixed schedule charges overnight and discharges at the evening peak; perfect insight is the ceiling. Sharpe and Sortino annualise the daily $/MW P&L (√365). Capture is the share of the perfect-insight profit.

The signal’s gross Sharpe (8.2) is higher than perfect insight’s (7.83): perfect insight earns more dollars only by taking spike-day variance.

Demand response: scarcity capture

Interruptible load curtails during the priciest ~2% of hours. Because the budget is spent only on the extreme tail, forecast ranking maps almost directly to dollars.

Carbon overlay: green is cheap

Running a flexible load in the cleanest forecast hours cuts CO₂ and saves money, because the carbon-optimal and cost-optimal hours are ~85–90% the same.

Forecast skill (t+24, walk-forward)

Day-ahead MAE and skill against a seasonal baseline (0 = no better than the baseline, 1 = perfect).

Methodology. Gradient-boosted trees forecast each hour 24 hours ahead, retrained on an expanding window with a gap the length of the forecast horizon so nothing leaks. Dispatch is scored on settled ERCOT day-ahead prices, after 85% round-trip efficiency and a $5/MWh cost. Every number here is walk-forward out of sample. None of it is live paper trading yet.

SYSTEMIC RISK & POSITIONING RANDOM MATRIX THEORY · CFTC COT · WEEKLY

Market structure

Two market-wide regime gauges that frame the environment our cross-asset book trades into. Random Matrix Theory measures how concentrated the S&P is into a single common factor. When it spikes, diversification breaks down just when it is needed most. CFTC Commitment of Traders shows how crowded large speculators are across equities, gold, rates and the dollar, positioning extremes that often precede reversals.

Systemic correlation: random matrix theory
Eigenspectrum of the S&P correlation matrix vs the Marchenko–Pastur noise band (q = N/T). Eigenvalues past λ+ are real factors; the largest (λ_max) is the “market mode”. A high market-mode share + collapsing factor count ⇒ everything moving as one (diversification breakdown).

Teal = λ_max share (correlation concentration). Orange = # significant factors. Share spikes + factor count collapsing ⇒ diversification breakdown.
